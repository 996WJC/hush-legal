Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 2026

Wornly ("Wornly", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Wornly app and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Wornly may collect the following types of information:

Information You Provide

  • Items you add to the app, including names, brands, categories, purchase prices, resale prices, notes, and related wardrobe details.
  • Wear log information, including dates and optional notes you choose to record.
  • Feedback or support messages you send to us by email.

Automatically Collected Information

We may receive limited technical information such as:

  • App version
  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Basic diagnostic information related to app stability and performance

Wornly does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information unless you choose to include it in notes, emails, or other content you submit.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate the app
  • Save and display your wardrobe and wear log data
  • Improve features, stability, and performance
  • Respond to feedback and support requests
  • Maintain security and prevent misuse

3. Data Storage

Your data may be stored locally on your device. If cloud sync or backup features are enabled now or in the future, your data may also be stored through third-party platform services such as Apple iCloud, depending on the features you use.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in the following limited situations:

  • With service providers or platform providers when necessary to operate app features
  • If required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • To protect the rights, safety, and security of Wornly, our users, or others

5. Third-Party Services

Wornly may rely on third-party services or platform features, including Apple services, for app distribution, payments, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, or cloud storage.

Those third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you use in connection with Wornly.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the app and related services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

If you delete data within the app, that data may be removed from the active app experience, although some technical backups or cached data may remain for a limited period where applicable.

7. Your Choices

You may:

  • Edit or delete your content within the app
  • Choose whether to send us feedback by email
  • Stop using the app at any time
  • Manage device-level permissions and settings through your Apple device settings

If future versions of Wornly include account-based services or cloud features, additional controls may become available.

8. Children's Privacy

Wornly is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

9. International Users

If you use Wornly outside your home country, you understand that your information may be processed in countries where privacy laws may differ from those in your jurisdiction.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the date at the top of this page and take other appropriate steps as required.
