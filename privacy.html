Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hush ("we", "our", or "us") handles your information when you use the Hush app ("App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Hush does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All data you enter — including cycle dates, symptoms, notes, and any other health information — is stored exclusively on your device and is never sent to our servers or any third party.

2. Health Data

Any health-related data you enter in the App remains on your device at all times. We have no access to this data. If you choose to enable iCloud backup on your device, your data may be backed up through Apple's iCloud service, which is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

3. Analytics and Tracking

Hush does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. We do not track your behavior within the App.

4. In-App Purchase

Hush offers a one-time purchase ("Hush Premium") processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information. All transactions are handled by Apple and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Children's Privacy

Hush is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us so we can address the situation.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: hushapp.support@gmail.com
