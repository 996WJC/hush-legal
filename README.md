## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How is my personal health data protected?
Hush is built with a **100% privacy-first and offline-first architecture**:
* We do **not** have user accounts, external databases, or third-party analytics SDKs.
* All cycle logs, symptoms, and notes are stored strictly on your local device.
* If iCloud is enabled on your device, data syncs exclusively via Apple’s private CloudKit. We never see or store your data.

### 2. How do I restore my lifetime purchase (Hush Premium)?
If you switch devices or reinstall Hush:
1. Open **Hush**.
2. Tap your profile avatar in the top-left corner to open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Hush Premium** (or the Paywall entry).
4. Tap **Restore Purchases** at the bottom. As long as you are signed in with the same Apple ID, your lifetime access will be restored immediately without any additional charge.

### 3. How do I export my health data for my doctor?
1. Open **Hush** and tap the **Insights** tab.
2. Scroll to the bottom and select **Full history export**.
3. Choose either **PDF Report** (formatted summary ready for medical appointments) or **CSV File** (raw data spreadsheet).

### 4. How does Apple Health integration work?
Hush supports bidirectional sync with Apple Health via Apple's official HealthKit framework:
* You can turn **Apple Health** on or off at any time in **Settings**.
* No data is ever transmitted outside your device and Apple Health ecosystem.
